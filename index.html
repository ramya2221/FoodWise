About this prototype: FoodWise is an educational CSP project demonstrating rule-based food habit analysis and practical suggestions. It does not diagnose medical or nutritional conditions.

About FoodWise

FoodWise aims to create awareness about everyday food habits. It helps users understand common eating problems and explore affordable, practical ways to improve their food choices.

Food Habit Problems & Solutions

🍟

Frequent Junk Food Consumption

Problem: Eating packaged snacks and junk food frequently.

Solution: Try homemade snacks, roasted chana, peanuts and seasonal fruits.

🥦

Low Fruit and Vegetable Intake

Problem: Not including enough fruits and vegetables in daily meals.

Solution: Include affordable seasonal fruits and vegetables regularly.

📚

Lack of Nutrition Awareness

Problem: Limited understanding of balanced nutrition.

Solution: Learn about food groups and include varied foods in meals.

⏰

Irregular Meal Timings

Problem: Eating meals at inconsistent times or frequently delaying meals.

Solution: Plan regular meal times and maintain a daily routine.

💧

Insufficient Water Intake

Problem: Forgetting to drink water throughout the day.

Solution: Keep drinking water regularly and carry a water bottle.
